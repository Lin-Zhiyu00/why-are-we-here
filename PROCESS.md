# Process Statement
I used two AI tools for this assignment: Doubao and DeepSeek.

The four main tasks I asked the AI to assist with were:
1. Analyse the requirements of Assignment 1
2. Identify and verify academic references for my essay
3. Revise and standardise APA‑7 and Markdown formatting for the document
4. Summarise the core arguments from the course video *The Art of Code*

Meanwhile, I explicitly rejected several functions suggested by the AI throughout the writing process:
1. Automatic full‑text translation
2. Adding extra descriptive sentences or expanding my original writing content
3. Additional creative rewriting or content‑related suggestions

All core ideas, personal reflection and the original wording of my essay were written independently by myself. AI was only used as a supporting tool for checking rules and formatting.
